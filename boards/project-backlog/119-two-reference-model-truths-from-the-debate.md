# Two reference-model truths from the debate

The debate on decisions 15 and 17 surfaced two model defects that stand whatever the rules say. NorthBank's `DecisioningApp.Decide` is described as "pull the bureau, run the scorecard, check affordability" and the `Scorecard` scores "an application and its bureau report", but no bureau exists: there is a `BureauReport` value object and an invariant that it is fresh, and no external context, no consumption. The Head of Credit Risk's words ("bureau report no older than thirty days; affordability at most forty-five percent; every decision carries reasons") do not say who pulls it, so today's shape, the front pulls and hands the report to the scorecard, preserves every word. And StreamLine's `TitleDetail` payload lists `seasons: Season[]` on every title including films, which is a false contract; its own comment says the wire carries a `kind`, which is fine, and `seasons` should be optional (decision 24).
